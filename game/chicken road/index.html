How to play?

  1. Choose your bet and difficulty.
  2. Press GO to send the chicken across the road.
  3. Each safe crossing increases the multiplier.
  4. The chicken can be shot down while crossing; the bet is lost.
  5. Cars stop when the chicken is resting on a tile.
  6. Press CASH OUT before the next crossing fails.
  7. Auto cash out stops the round at your selected multiplier.
