JetX Game Rules & Instructions

How to Play JetX:

  • Place one or multiple bets before the jet takes off.
  • As the jet takes flight, the odds multiplier increases exponentially starting from 1.00x.
  • Click Cash Out before the jet flies away ("FLEW AWAY") to collect your winnings: Payout = Bet × Multiplier.
  • If the jet flies away before you cash out, the bet is lost.

Game Limits & Provably Fair:

  • Minimum Bet: 1.00 ETB
  • Maximum Bet: 1,000.00 ETB
  • Maximum Multiplier: 25,000.00x
  • RTP (Return to Player): ~97.00%
  • Fairness: Every round crash point is predetermined using HMAC-SHA256.
